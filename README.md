## Favorite Movie: The Notebook

**The Notebook** (2004) is a romantic drama film directed by *Nick Cassavetes* and based on the novel by *Nicholas Sparks*.

### Why It's My Favorite
- Emotional and timeless love story between Noah and Allie
- Strong performances by Ryan Gosling and Rachel McAdams
- Unforgettable rain scene and ending

> "The best love is the kind that awakens the soul and makes us reach for more."
